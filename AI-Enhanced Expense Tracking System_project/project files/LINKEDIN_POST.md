# 🚀 Introducing AI Expense Tracking - Your AI-Powered Expense Tracker

I'm excited to share **AI Expense Tracking**, a modern Progressive Web App (PWA) that transforms how you track and manage your finances! 💰

## ✨ Key Features

🤖 **AI-Powered Receipt Scanning**
- Simply snap a photo of your receipt and let Google Gemini AI extract transaction details automatically
- No more manual data entry!

📊 **Smart Analytics & Insights**
- Visual charts showing your spending patterns
- AI-generated personalized financial advice
- Category-wise expense breakdown

🎮 **Gamification**
- Earn points and unlock achievements as you track expenses
- Build streaks to stay consistent
- Unlock new avatars as you progress

💰 **Budget Management**
- Set weekly, monthly, or yearly budget limits
- Category-specific budget controls
- Real-time budget tracking and alerts

📱 **Progressive Web App (PWA)**
- Install directly on your phone or desktop
- Works offline with local storage
- No app store needed!

📤 **Data Export**
- Export your transactions to PDF or Excel
- Full control over your financial data
